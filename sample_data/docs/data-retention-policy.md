---
title: Data Retention and Disposal Policy
type: policy
source: compliance
date: 2025-02-01
---

This policy defines how long company data is retained and how it is disposed of. Customer transaction records are retained for seven years to satisfy financial regulations, then securely deleted. Application logs are retained for 13 months and then purged automatically.

Email is retained for three years from receipt. Draft documents and temporary files should be deleted by their owners after 90 days. Backups are kept on a 35-day rotation and are not archives; deleted production data disappears from backups as they expire.

Legal holds override all retention schedules. When Legal issues a hold, deletion of the held data is suspended until the hold is released in writing, regardless of the retention period that would otherwise apply.

Disposal of storage media must use cryptographic erasure or physical destruction, and the IT asset team records a certificate of destruction for every device.
