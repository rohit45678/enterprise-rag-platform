Welcome to the company. On your first day, HR will verify your identity documents and issue your employee badge. Your manager will introduce you to the team and assign an onboarding buddy for your first two weeks.

IT provisions your laptop, monitor, and headset before your start date. Your network account, email mailbox, and VPN access are created automatically from the HR system. You will set your password and register for multi-factor authentication during the first morning with IT support present.

During your first week you must complete the security awareness training, the code of conduct acknowledgment, and your role-specific training plan. Your onboarding checklist in the HR portal tracks each item, and your manager reviews progress with you on day five.

Payroll setup requires your bank details and tax forms to be submitted in the HR portal by the end of your first week. Benefits enrollment opens on your start date and closes 30 days later.
