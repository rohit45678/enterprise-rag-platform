---
title: Expense Reimbursement Policy
type: policy
source: finance
date: 2024-11-20
---

Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing company duties. Every expense claim must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt attached for any expense over 25 dollars.

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to a per diem of 75 dollars per day. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Hotel expenses are capped at 220 dollars per night unless a conference rate was pre-approved by Finance.

Expenses up to 500 dollars are approved by your direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5000 dollars require approval from a department director, and expenses above 5000 dollars require Finance approval before purchase.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after approval. Claims submitted without receipts, or later than 30 days, will be denied unless the Finance team grants an exception in writing.
