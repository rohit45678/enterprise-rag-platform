This runbook defines how production incidents are declared, escalated, and resolved. Any employee who detects a service outage or a security incident opens an incident ticket and pages the on-call engineer.

Severity levels are defined as follows. Severity 1 is a complete outage of a customer-facing service or an active security breach. Severity 2 is a partial outage or major degradation. Severity 3 covers minor issues with a workaround, and Severity 4 is informational.

For a Severity 1 incident, the incident commander is paged immediately, a bridge call is opened within 15 minutes, and executives are notified. Status updates are posted every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved. Severity 2 incidents are acknowledged within 30 minutes and updated hourly.

After resolution, the incident commander schedules a blameless postmortem within five business days. The postmortem records the timeline, the root cause, and follow-up action items with owners and due dates.
